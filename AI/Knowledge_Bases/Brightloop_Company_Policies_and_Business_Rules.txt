BRIGHTLOOP SOFTWARE
COMPANY POLICIES & BUSINESS RULES
Hackathon Edition

Document purpose
This document defines the proposed business policies and operational rules for Brightloop Software's Subscription Management System. It is based on the supplied hackathon problem statement and the policy outline reviewed with the team.

Interpretation
- "Specified" means the rule is directly stated in the hackathon brief or the supplied Brightloop policy draft.
- "Recommended" means a practical rule proposed to make the system complete and reliable; it is not explicitly mandated by the brief.
- "To be defined" identifies a business decision the team should confirm before implementation.
- Where a rule is not specified, do not treat an assumption as an approved company policy.

1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

1.1 About Brightloop Software
Brightloop Software is a software company that provides subscription-based software services to businesses.

1.2 Nature of Business
Brightloop earns revenue by providing products and services through recurring subscriptions. Customers may subscribe on a daily, weekly, monthly or yearly billing frequency, depending on the plan.

1.3 Customer Categories
Brightloop serves customers ranging from small businesses on monthly plans to large companies on yearly contracts.

1.4 Products, Plans and Add-ons
The company offers software, services, add-ons and other products. Products may have variants, such as a brand, that change the product price. Plans define recurring pricing, billing frequency, minimum quantity, availability and permitted subscription actions.

1.5 Subscription Operations
The system must support the subscription lifecycle from quotation and confirmation through activation, recurring billing, payment collection, renewal and closure.

1.6 Customer Support
Customers may contact Brightloop's support team for subscription-related and billing-related assistance. The support team should verify the relevant customer, subscription or invoice information before providing assistance.

1.7 Internal Responsibilities
Administrators configure the system. Internal staff handle day-to-day customer and subscription operations within assigned permissions. Sales representatives prepare quotations and subscription offers. Finance personnel review billing, payments and revenue information.


2. USER REGISTRATION, AUTHENTICATION AND ACCOUNT POLICIES

2.1 Customer Registration [Specified]
- A customer can create an account using their name, email address and password.
- Each email address can belong to only one account.
- Mandatory registration fields must be validated before an account is created.

2.2 Password Requirements [Specified]
- Passwords must contain more than eight characters.
- Passwords must include at least one uppercase letter.
- Passwords must include at least one lowercase letter.
- Passwords must include at least one special character.

2.3 Password Storage [Recommended]
- Passwords must never be stored in plain text.
- Passwords must be stored using a suitable secure password-hashing method.

2.4 Login [Specified / Recommended]
- Users must authenticate before accessing protected system areas.
- Access must be restricted according to the user's role.
- Inactive accounts should not be permitted to log in.

2.5 Password Recovery [Specified]
- A user who forgets their password can request a reset link.
- The reset link must be sent to the user's registered email address.
- Reset links should expire after a defined period. The expiry duration is to be defined.
- Reset tokens should be stored securely and should not be stored in plain text.

2.6 Account Management [Recommended]
- Account activation and deactivation must be controlled.
- Account details should be updated only by an authorized user.
- Customers must only access information belonging to their own account.

2.7 Staff Account Creation [Specified]
- Only an administrator can create a new staff account.
- Staff members cannot create additional staff accounts.
- The system should record which administrator created each staff account.


3. USER ROLES, PERMISSIONS AND ACCESS CONTROL

3.1 Role-Based Access
The system has three primary roles:
- Admin: controls the system and its configuration.
- Staff: performs day-to-day operational work with limited access.
- Customer: accesses their own information through the customer portal.

3.2 Administrator Responsibilities [Specified]
- Manage products and product variants.
- Create and manage plans.
- Configure tax rates.
- Create and manage discount rules.
- Create staff accounts.
- Control system-wide configuration and access.

3.3 Staff Responsibilities [Specified at a high level]
- Handle day-to-day customer and subscription operations.
- Prepare quotations and subscription offers.
- Work with invoices and payment records where permission is granted.
- Assist customers using verified subscription and invoice information.
- Staff permissions must remain limited and must not include administrator-only configuration by default.

3.4 Customer Responsibilities [Specified]
- Register and log in.
- View their own subscriptions and invoices.
- Access permitted subscription-management actions through the customer portal.
- View relevant payment and outstanding-balance information.

3.5 Access Restrictions [Specified / Recommended]
- Customers must not view another customer's records.
- Staff must not access administrator-only functions.
- Every protected action must check the user's role and authorization on the server side.
- Hiding a button in the user interface is not sufficient access control.

3.6 Staff Permission Matrix [To Be Defined]
The exact permissions for different staff functions should be confirmed by the team. The problem statement specifies limited staff access but does not provide a complete staff-level permission matrix.

3.7 Activity Tracking [Recommended]
Record important administrative and financial actions, including the user who performed the action and its timestamp.


4. CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT POLICIES

4.1 Customer Records
A customer record should contain the customer's name, company name where applicable, email, phone number, tax number or GSTIN where applicable, address and payment terms.

4.2 Customer and Login Account Relationship
A customer business record and a user login account are related but distinct records. A customer record may exist without a customer portal login.

4.3 Customer Email
Customer contact email should be maintained accurately for business communication. Account email uniqueness must be enforced for login accounts.

4.4 Payment Terms
Payment terms define the number of days allowed for payment after an invoice date. The system should use the applicable customer or subscription payment terms when determining invoice due dates.

4.5 Customer History
Authorized staff should be able to review a customer's subscriptions, invoices and recorded payments.

4.6 Customer Information Updates
Customer information should be updated by authorized users. Changes to customer details must not silently rewrite historical invoice information.

4.7 Customer Activation and Deactivation [Recommended]
The system should support controlled account deactivation. The treatment of active subscriptions and outstanding invoices when a customer account is deactivated is to be defined.

4.8 Data Access
Customers may access only their own profile, subscriptions, invoices and payment history.


5. PRODUCT MANAGEMENT POLICIES

5.1 Product Creation [Specified]
The administrator can create products with:
- Product name.
- Product type.
- Sales price.
- Cost price.
- Notes or description.
- Applicable default tax configuration.

5.2 Supported Product Types [Specified]
- Software.
- Service.
- Add-on.
- Other.

5.3 Product Pricing
- Sales price is the price charged to the customer.
- Cost price is maintained separately from sales price.
- Prices must be non-negative.
- The system should use a consistent currency and precision for monetary calculations.

5.4 Product Variants [Specified]
- A product may have variants based on attributes such as brand.
- Each variant may have a price adjustment.
- The final variant price is calculated from the base sales price plus the applicable price adjustment.

Example:
Base product price: Rs. 2,000
Variant: Odoo
Variant price adjustment: Rs. 560
Resulting price: Rs. 2,560

5.5 Product Tax
A product may have a default tax rate. The applicable tax for a subscription or invoice line must be recorded according to the configured tax rules.

5.6 Product Activation and Deactivation [Recommended]
Inactive products should not be offered for new subscriptions or quotations. The treatment of products already used in existing subscriptions must preserve historical records.

5.7 Product Modification
Changes to a product's current price or description should not silently change previously confirmed subscription or invoice line values.

5.8 Product Deletion
Products referenced by existing plans, subscriptions or invoices should not be physically deleted if doing so would damage historical records. Deactivation is the recommended approach.


6. PLAN MANAGEMENT POLICIES

6.1 Plan Creation [Specified]
A plan should contain:
- Plan name.
- Price.
- Billing frequency.
- Billing interval.
- Minimum quantity.
- Availability start date.
- Availability end date.
- Subscription behavior settings.

6.2 Supported Billing Frequencies [Specified]
- Daily.
- Weekly.
- Monthly.
- Yearly.

6.3 Billing Interval [Recommended]
Billing frequency and billing interval should be separate settings. For example, a monthly frequency with an interval of two means billing every two months.

6.4 Minimum Quantity [Specified]
The quantity selected for a plan or its applicable product lines must satisfy the configured minimum quantity.

6.5 Plan Availability [Specified]
Plans may have an availability start date and end date. A plan should only be offered for new subscriptions during its configured availability period.

6.6 Plan-Level Subscription Rules [Specified]
The administrator can configure the following separately for each plan:
- Whether subscriptions close automatically upon expiry.
- Whether early cancellation is permitted.
- Whether pausing is permitted.
- Whether renewal is permitted.

6.7 Plan Product Lines
- A plan may contain multiple products.
- Each plan line must specify a product, optional variant, quantity and unit price.
- Plan product lines should be copied into a newly created subscription.
- Subscription lines should be independently recorded so that later plan edits do not silently rewrite an existing subscription.

6.8 Plan Activation and Deactivation [Recommended]
Inactive plans should not be offered for new subscriptions. Existing subscriptions should retain their recorded plan association and historical terms.

6.9 Plan Changes [To Be Defined]
The team must decide whether changes to a plan's price or included products affect existing subscriptions or only new subscriptions.


7. QUOTATION AND QUOTATION TEMPLATE POLICIES

7.1 Quotation Templates [Specified]
- The system should allow quotation templates to be created.
- Each template should have a name and an associated plan.
- Templates may contain product lines, variants, quantities and unit prices.
- Each template must have a validity period expressed in days.
- Templates can be activated or deactivated.

7.2 Quotation Creation [Specified]
- A sales representative can prepare an offer for a customer.
- The representative may start from an existing quotation template.
- The selected template should populate the associated plan and product lines.
- The quotation must be associated with a customer.
- The quotation should have a validity end date.

7.3 Quotation Validity
The quotation validity end date should be calculated using the quotation date and the template's validity period.

7.4 Quotation Lifecycle
A quotation is represented as a subscription record in the Quotation status in the proposed hackathon design. It is not necessarily a separate database entity.

7.5 Quotation Modification [Recommended]
Authorized users may modify a draft quotation. Changes after confirmation should be controlled and must not rewrite historical records without an explicit process.

7.6 Quotation Expiry
The system should identify expired quotations and prevent them from being treated as valid offers. Whether an expired quotation can be reactivated or must be recreated is to be defined.

7.7 Quotation Confirmation
A valid quotation may proceed through confirmation according to the subscription lifecycle and authorized workflow.


8. SUBSCRIPTION MANAGEMENT POLICIES

8.1 Subscription Creation
Each subscription must be associated with:
- A unique subscription number.
- A customer.
- A plan.
- A start date.
- An expiry date, where applicable.
- Payment terms.
- Product and variant lines.
- Quantities and unit prices.
- Applicable tax rates.

8.2 Subscription Number
Every subscription must have a unique reference number. The proposed format is SUB-00001, with a sequential numeric portion.

8.3 Subscription Lifecycle [Specified]
Primary lifecycle:
Draft -> Quotation -> Confirmed -> Active -> Closed

Other possible statuses:
- Paused.
- Cancelled.
- Expired.

8.4 Status Transition Validation [Recommended]
The system must validate status transitions and prevent invalid changes. The allowed transitions should be consistently enforced in the application and database.

8.5 Activation
A confirmed subscription may become active. Its next billing date must be initialized according to its billing schedule.

8.6 Recurring Billing
Active subscriptions should be considered for invoice generation when their next billing date is due. Paused, cancelled, expired or closed subscriptions must not be billed as active subscriptions unless a specifically approved rule says otherwise.

8.7 Renewal [Specified]
- Renewal is permitted only if the associated plan allows renewal.
- A renewed subscription should reference the previous subscription.
- The new subscription period and billing schedule must be established.
- The renewal workflow must not create duplicate billing.

8.8 Pause [Specified]
- A subscription can be paused only if its plan allows pausing.
- A paused subscription must not continue normal recurring billing unless the business explicitly defines otherwise.
- Resumption should return an eligible subscription to Active.

8.9 Early Cancellation [Specified]
- Early cancellation is permitted only when the associated plan allows it.
- Cancellation should be performed by an authorized user or through a permitted customer action.
- A cancellation reason should be recorded where applicable.

8.10 Expiry and Closure [Specified]
- Subscriptions follow their configured expiry dates and automatic closure policies.
- Plans may specify automatic closure upon expiry.
- Expired subscriptions should not continue normal recurring billing.

8.11 Subscription Product Lines
Each subscription line should preserve its description, product reference where applicable, variant, quantity, unit price, applicable tax and active/inactive state.

8.12 Subscription Modification [Recommended]
Changes to subscription lines, quantities, prices and add-ons must follow an explicit business rule. Changes must not silently modify invoices already generated.

8.13 Subscription Status History [Recommended]
The system should record status changes, including the previous status, new status, subscription reference and timestamp.

8.14 Subscription Visibility
Authorized internal team members should be able to see the current subscription status. Customers should be able to view their own subscriptions through the portal.


9. ADD-ON MANAGEMENT POLICIES

9.1 Add-on Products
Add-ons are products that may be enabled or disabled in connection with a customer's subscription.

9.2 Add-on Availability
Only add-ons permitted for the relevant plan or subscription should be offered.

9.3 Add-on Activation
The system should record when an add-on is activated, its quantity and its applicable price and tax.

9.4 Add-on Deactivation
The system should record when an add-on is deactivated. Deactivation should not delete historical invoice or subscription records.

9.5 Add-on Billing [To Be Defined]
The team must decide whether an add-on change takes effect immediately or from the next billing cycle, and whether prorated charges or credits apply.

9.6 Add-on History [Recommended]
Changes to add-ons should be traceable, including activation, deactivation, quantity and effective date.


10. BILLING AND INVOICE MANAGEMENT POLICIES

10.1 Automatic Invoice Generation [Specified]
Invoices must be generated from subscriptions according to their billing schedules. The system should populate invoice details from the associated customer and subscription.

10.2 Billing Schedule
Billing frequency and interval determine when recurring invoices become due for generation.

10.3 Billing Period
Each recurring invoice should identify the billing period it covers through a period start date and end date.

10.4 Invoice Number
Every invoice must have a unique invoice number. The proposed format is INV-00001.

10.5 Invoice Information [Specified]
An invoice should contain:
- Invoice number.
- Customer name and contact details.
- Customer address and tax number where applicable.
- Associated subscription.
- Invoice date and due date.
- Billing period.
- Product descriptions.
- Quantities.
- Unit prices.
- Discounts.
- Tax names and rates.
- Tax amounts.
- Subtotal.
- Discount total.
- Tax total.
- Final payable amount.
- Amount paid.
- Outstanding balance.

10.6 Invoice Lifecycle [Specified]
- Draft.
- Confirmed.
- Paid.
- Cancelled.

10.7 Invoice Confirmation
Authorized staff can confirm a draft invoice. Once confirmed, its financial line details should be protected from casual modification.

10.8 Invoice Sending and Printing [Specified]
The team should be able to send and print invoices.

10.9 Invoice Cancellation [Specified]
Authorized users should be able to cancel an invoice according to the permitted invoice lifecycle. Cancellation should be recorded and must not erase the historical transaction.

10.10 Invoice Due Date
The due date should be calculated using the invoice date and applicable payment terms.

10.11 Duplicate Invoice Prevention [Specified by the business objective]
The system must not generate more than one live invoice for the same subscription and billing period.

10.12 Missed Billing Prevention
The system should identify active subscriptions due for billing and provide a reliable way to process them. Failed invoice generation should be visible and safely retryable.

10.13 Invoice History
Invoice details and historical financial values must remain traceable.

10.14 Invoice Modification
Draft invoices may be edited by authorized users. Confirmed invoices should have their lines and financial calculations locked. Corrections should use an explicitly defined cancellation or adjustment process.

10.15 Invoice Generation Safety [Recommended]
Invoice creation should be safe to retry. Re-running a billing operation must not create a duplicate invoice for a period already billed.


11. DISCOUNT MANAGEMENT POLICIES

11.1 Discount Creation [Specified]
Only administrators can create discount rules.

11.2 Supported Discount Types [Specified]
- Fixed discount: a fixed monetary amount is deducted from the eligible amount.
- Percentage discount: a percentage of the eligible amount is deducted.

11.3 Discount Eligibility [Specified]
Eligibility may depend on:
- Minimum purchase amount.
- Minimum quantity.
- Validity start date.
- Validity end date.
- Usage limit.

11.4 Discount Applicability [Specified]
A discount may apply to:
- A specific product.
- An entire subscription.

11.5 Discount Activation
Only active discounts within their validity period and with remaining usage capacity may be applied.

11.6 Automatic Application [Specified]
Eligible discounts should be applied automatically to the next applicable invoice without requiring staff to remember to apply them manually.

11.7 Discount Calculation
The system must calculate the discount against the eligible product or subscription amount. The discount must not reduce the eligible amount below zero.

11.8 Usage Tracking
The system should track how many times a discount has been used and prevent use beyond its configured limit.

11.9 Expiry and Exhaustion [Specified]
A discount must stop being applied when it expires, is deactivated or reaches its usage limit.

11.10 Discount History [Recommended]
Invoices should preserve the discount applied and the amount deducted so that historical totals remain explainable.

11.11 Discount Stacking [To Be Defined]
The team must decide whether multiple discounts may be applied to the same invoice or line and, if so, in what order.


12. TAX MANAGEMENT POLICIES

12.1 Tax Configuration
Administrators configure the tax rates available to products and subscription lines.

12.2 Configured Tax Rates [Specified]
Examples include:
- GST 18%.
- GST 5%.
- No Tax.

12.3 Tax Application [Specified]
Product and subscription invoice lines may have applicable tax rates.

12.4 Automatic Tax Calculation [Specified]
Taxes must be calculated automatically using the configured tax rate associated with the relevant line.

12.5 Taxable Amount
The system must identify the amount to which a tax rate applies. The proposed calculation applies discounts before calculating tax on the remaining taxable amount.

12.6 Tax Display
Tax amounts should be shown separately from the taxable amount and included in the final payable amount.

12.7 Tax Snapshot [Recommended]
The invoice should preserve the tax name and rate used when it was generated. A later change to a configured tax rate must not silently alter an existing invoice.

12.8 Tax Rounding [To Be Defined]
The team must select a consistent rounding rule and apply it across invoice lines and totals.

12.9 Tax Type and Jurisdiction [To Be Defined]
The supplied brief gives example GST rates but does not specify intra-state/inter-state GST splitting, place-of-supply rules or other jurisdiction-specific tax handling. Do not imply that these are implemented unless the team defines them.


13. PAYMENT MANAGEMENT POLICIES

13.1 Payment Recording [Specified]
Payments must be recorded against individual invoices.

13.2 Payment Information [Specified]
Each payment record should contain:
- Unique payment number.
- Invoice reference.
- Customer reference.
- Payment method.
- Payment amount.
- Payment date.
- Note.
- Staff member who recorded the payment, where applicable.

13.3 Payment Number
Every payment should have a unique payment reference. The proposed format is PAY-00001.

13.4 Supported Payment Methods [Specified]
- Cash.
- Bank Transfer.
- Card.
- UPI.
- Cheque.
- Other.

13.5 Partial Payments [Specified]
An invoice may receive multiple partial payments.

13.6 Payment Completion [Specified]
An invoice is fully paid when the total recorded payments equal its payable amount. Its status should then change to Paid.

13.7 Outstanding Balance
Outstanding balance is the invoice payable amount minus the total payments recorded against it.

13.8 Payment Validation [Recommended]
- Payments must have a positive amount.
- A payment must belong to the same customer as the invoice.
- Payments should only be recorded against invoices eligible to receive payment.
- A payment must not exceed the remaining invoice balance unless an approved overpayment process exists.

13.9 Payment History
The system should show all payments associated with an invoice and customer.

13.10 Payment Reconciliation
Staff should be able to identify the invoice associated with each recorded payment and verify the payment details.

13.11 Payment Corrections [To Be Defined]
The brief does not specify refunds, reversals, failed cheques or correction of incorrectly recorded payments. The team must define whether these are in scope and how they affect invoice balances.


14. OUTSTANDING PAYMENTS, OVERDUE INVOICES AND COLLECTIONS

14.1 Outstanding Invoice Tracking
The system must show invoices that have not been fully paid.

14.2 Partially Paid Invoices
An invoice with one or more payments but a remaining balance should be identified as partially paid.

14.3 Overdue Invoice Identification
An invoice is overdue when its due date has passed and an outstanding balance remains.

14.4 Days Overdue
The system may calculate the number of days between the due date and the current date for overdue invoices.

14.5 Outstanding Amount
The outstanding amount is calculated as the invoice total minus payments received.

14.6 Overdue Amount
The overdue amount is the sum of outstanding balances for invoices whose due dates have passed.

14.7 Payment Follow-up [Recommended]
Authorized staff should be able to identify customers with overdue invoices and follow up for payment.

14.8 Collections History [Recommended]
The system should retain relevant payment and follow-up information where implemented.

14.9 Display Status Versus Stored Status
Partially Paid and Overdue may be calculated display states. The underlying invoice status can remain Confirmed until the full amount is received. Paid and Cancelled remain invoice lifecycle statuses.


15. CUSTOMER SELF-SERVICE PORTAL POLICIES

15.1 Customer Access [Specified]
Customers should have a portal through which they can view and manage their own subscriptions without needing to email Brightloop for every routine action.

15.2 Customer Dashboard [Recommended]
The portal should summarize the customer's subscriptions, invoices and payment information.

15.3 Subscription Visibility [Specified]
Customers may view their own:
- Subscription numbers.
- Plans.
- Subscription status.
- Start and expiry dates.
- Subscribed products and add-ons.
- Relevant renewal information.

15.4 Subscription Actions
Customers may perform or request only those actions permitted by the associated plan and the portal's access rules. These may include eligible pause, resume, renewal or cancellation actions.

15.5 Invoice Visibility [Specified]
Customers may view their own invoices and relevant payment status.

15.6 Invoice Download and Printing [Recommended]
The portal should allow customers to download or print their own invoices if this capability is implemented.

15.7 Payment History
Customers should be able to view payments recorded against their own invoices.

15.8 Outstanding Balance
Customers should be able to see outstanding balances and overdue invoices associated with their account.

15.9 Customer Data Isolation [Specified]
A customer must never be able to view another customer's profile, subscriptions, invoices or payment history.

The finance lead needs one place to review active subscriptions, revenue, payments and overdue invoices.

- Subscriptions by plan.

16.5 Revenue Reports [Specified / Recommended]
- Revenue collected by month.

16.6 Payment Reports [Recommended]

16.10 Invoiced Versus Collected Revenue

17. NOTIFICATIONS, COMMUNICATION AND CUSTOMER SUPPORT

17.1 Password Reset Email [Specified]
A password reset link must be sent to the registered email address.

17.2 Invoice Communication [Specified]
The team must be able to send invoices.

17.3 Additional Notifications [Recommended]
If notification functionality is implemented, useful events include:
- Quotation created or approaching expiry.
- Subscription confirmed or activated.
- Subscription approaching expiry.
- Renewal due.
- Subscription paused, resumed or cancelled.
- Invoice generated or sent.
- Payment received.
- Invoice partially paid.
- Invoice overdue.

17.4 Notification Channel [To Be Defined]
The problem statement does not specify whether notifications must use email, in-app messages or both. The team should select the channel based on implementation time and available services.

17.5 Customer Support
- Customers may contact Brightloop support for subscription and billing assistance.
- Support personnel should verify the customer's identity and relevant records.
- Support responses should be based on the correct subscription or invoice information.
- Recording support interactions is a recommended enhancement.


18. SYSTEM SECURITY, DATA INTEGRITY AND PERFORMANCE

18.1 Authentication
Protected system functionality must require an authenticated user.

18.2 Authorization
Every protected operation must verify the user's role and permissions.

18.3 Password Security
Passwords must be securely hashed and never stored in plain text.

18.4 Customer Data Isolation
Customer-facing requests must be scoped to the authenticated customer's own records.

18.5 Unique Records
The system must enforce uniqueness for account emails and generated subscription, invoice and payment numbers.

18.6 Referential Integrity
Subscriptions, invoices, payments, customers, products and plans must maintain valid relationships.

18.7 Financial Accuracy
All financial calculations must use consistent precision and rounding rules. The proposed technical design uses integer paise for INR amounts.

18.8 Transaction Safety
Operations that update financial records should be performed safely so that partial failures do not leave inconsistent records.

18.9 Duplicate Billing Prevention
A subscription must not be invoiced twice for the same billing period, including when a billing job is retried or run concurrently.

18.10 Payment Integrity
Payment records must be validated against the relevant invoice and customer. The invoice balance and payment status must remain consistent.

18.11 Status Validation
Subscription and invoice status transitions must follow defined lifecycle rules.

18.12 Historical Data
Historical invoices and payments must remain traceable. Later changes to products, plans, discounts or taxes must not silently rewrite completed financial records.

18.13 Audit Trail [Recommended]
Important changes to subscriptions, invoices, payments, discounts and system configuration should be traceable to the responsible user and timestamp.

18.14 Error Handling
Failed billing, payment-recording and other critical operations should provide clear errors and should not create duplicate or partially committed financial records.

18.15 Performance [Specified]
Every page should respond in under two seconds.

18.16 Scalability [Specified]
The system should remain responsive with thousands of subscriptions.

18.17 Bulk Billing [Specified by scenario]
The system should handle hundreds of subscriptions due for billing at once without requiring manual invoice entry.

18.18 Database Optimization [Recommended]
Use appropriate indexes, efficient queries and summary calculations to support dashboard and operational performance.

18.19 Backup and Recovery [Optional Enhancement]
A backup and recovery process is recommended but is not explicitly required by the supplied problem statement.

APPENDIX B. STATUS DEFINITIONS

Subscription statuses:
- Draft: Subscription record is being prepared.
- Quotation: Subscription is being presented as an offer to the customer.
- Confirmed: The offer has been accepted and is awaiting activation or its effective start.
- Active: Subscription is currently running and eligible for scheduled billing.
- Paused: Subscription is temporarily suspended according to its plan rules.
- Cancelled: Subscription has been cancelled.
- Expired: Subscription has reached its expiry condition.
- Closed: Subscription lifecycle has been closed.

Invoice statuses:
- Draft: Invoice is being prepared and may be edited by authorized users.
- Confirmed: Invoice has been confirmed and is awaiting full payment.
- Paid: The full payable amount has been recorded as paid.
- Cancelled: Invoice has been cancelled.

Calculated invoice display states:
- Partially Paid: At least one payment has been received, but a balance remains.
- Overdue: The due date has passed and a balance remains.
- Outstanding: Any unpaid balance remains.

These calculated display states need not be stored as permanent invoice lifecycle statuses.


APPENDIX C. FINANCIAL AND BILLING RULES

C1. Line Amount
Line amount before discount and tax = quantity multiplied by unit price.

C2. Discounted Taxable Amount
Taxable amount = eligible line amount minus applicable discount.

C3. Tax Amount
Tax amount = taxable amount multiplied by the applicable tax rate.

C4. Invoice Total
Invoice total = subtotal minus discount total plus tax total.

C5. Amount Paid
Amount paid = sum of valid payment records associated with the invoice.

C6. Outstanding Balance
Outstanding balance = invoice total minus amount paid.

C7. Full Payment
An invoice is fully paid when the recorded payments equal the payable amount.

C8. Due Date
Due date = invoice date plus the applicable payment terms.

C9. Variant Price
Variant price = base product sales price plus variant price adjustment.

C10. Currency and Precision
The proposed hackathon implementation uses INR and stores money as integer paise. For example, Rs. 560.00 is stored as 56000 paise. The user interface should display amounts in rupees.

C11. Tax Rounding
The team must define whether tax is rounded per line or at invoice level and apply that method consistently.

APPENDIX E. GLOSSARY

Admin: User with authority to configure and control the system.

Staff: Internal user with limited operational permissions.

Customer: Business or individual purchasing Brightloop subscription services.

Product: A software product, service, add-on or other item offered by Brightloop.

Variant: A product option, such as a brand, that may change the product price.

Plan: A recurring commercial offering defining price, billing frequency, quantity requirements, availability and subscription rules.

Quotation Template: A reusable configuration containing a plan, product lines and quotation validity period.

Quotation: An offer prepared for a customer. In the proposed hackathon design, it is represented by a subscription in Quotation status.

Subscription: A customer's agreement to receive products or services under a selected plan for a defined period and billing arrangement.

Add-on: An additional product or service that can be enabled or disabled in connection with a subscription.

Billing Frequency: How often a subscription is billed: daily, weekly, monthly or yearly.

Billing Interval: The number of frequency units between billing events.

Invoice: A financial document generated for products or services supplied during a billing period.

Partial Payment: A payment that covers only part of an invoice's payable amount.

Outstanding Balance: The amount of an invoice that remains unpaid.

Overdue Invoice: An invoice with an outstanding balance whose due date has passed.

Discount: A fixed or percentage reduction applied when its eligibility rules are satisfied.

Tax Rate: The configured percentage used to calculate tax on an eligible taxable amount.

Revenue Collected: Money actually received and recorded through payments.

Invoiced Amount: The amount charged through invoices, whether or not it has been paid.

Audit Trail: A record of important actions, including who performed them and when.
