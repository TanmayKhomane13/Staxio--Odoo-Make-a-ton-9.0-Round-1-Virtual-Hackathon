Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Brightloop subscription software.

How are new customer accounts created?

Small businesses sign up online with email and a secure password (>8 chars with uppercase, lowercase, and special character). Each email is unique to a single account with automated password reset links.

Can plans be paused, closed early, or renewed?

Yes! Admins decide plan by plan whether they close automatically upon ending or can be paused, renewed, or closed early by authorized team members.

How do discount rules apply to invoices?

Admins create fixed amount or percentage discounts with purchase minimums and usage limits. Once applied, discounts automatically appear on the next invoice without manual tracking.
